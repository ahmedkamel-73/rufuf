# Rufuf Monetization — Final



Revenue is split conceptually into:
1. Platform ads: owned/managed by Rufuf.
2. Publisher ads: publisher supplies their own ad code and it is rendered only in their approved content.

Readers never pay to read books or articles.

Publisher ad code must be sanitized/validated according to the ad provider's policy. Do not execute arbitrary scripts supplied by untrusted users without an allowlist or isolated rendering strategy.
